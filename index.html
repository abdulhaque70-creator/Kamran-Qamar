About

Market Entry & Business Models ASEAN & China Commercial Steering Organisational Transformation JV Stakeholder Management Product Development Retail & Distribution

I am an international automotive executive with more than 20 years at Volkswagen and Daimler, working across ASEAN, China and European markets.

At Volkswagen AG in Wolfsburg, I am Business Manager for Malaysia and lead Business Development for ASEAN. I develop market-specific business models with local partners and lead cross-functional, cross-brand programmes. I have also led the restructuring and repositioning of Volkswagen's operations in Malaysia and Hong Kong.

Before that, I spent four years in Beijing building Volkswagen Finance's after-sales product portfolio across the Group's China networks and joint ventures. My career began at Daimler with business cases, transfer pricing and market-entry work on four continents.
