Methodology & model notes
Forward calc
CIF = FX × kit price. Ex-works / importer price = CIF + import duty + localisation + assembly + transport + other ex-works + VGM fixed + tax. Distributor margin = ex-works × md/(1−md). Dealer margin = (ex-works + warranty + other + distributor margin) × mr/(1−mr). Retail = sum of all.
Reverse calc
Max ex-works = (target × (1−mr) − warranty − other) × (1−md). Max CIF = max ex-works − local costs and tax. Max kit price = max CIF ÷ FX.
Tax
Tax = reference tax × (1 − reduction) ÷ (1 − reference reduction). This is an approximation for scenarios only and has not been checked against Malaysian excise or sales-tax law.
Margins
Distributor and dealer margins are treated as gross margin on their own selling price. The original may use fixed amounts or a different margin base.
Volume
Annual volume isn't in the source workbook. It only drives the annual kit value (kit price × volume).
Validate before use
Confirm the taxable OMV base, how the ED/ST reduction is applied, assembly cost scope, the 6% importer margin base, the 5% dealer margin base, and whether VAT/SST applies.

Simplified demonstration model for illustration. Not a validated Malaysian tax or CKD business-case calculation.
